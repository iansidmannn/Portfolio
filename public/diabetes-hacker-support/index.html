Frequently asked questions

What does Diabetes Hacker actually do?

You snap a photo of your meal, enter how many units you bolused, and the app automatically pulls your blood glucose from Dexcom over the next 3 hours. The journal then shows you — for every meal — what happened to your blood sugar. Over time you build a real memory of how foods affect you.

Does this app tell me what to bolus?

No. Diabetes Hacker is a logging and tracking tool. It does not recommend doses, suggest changes, or give medical advice. It shows you what happened so you (with your healthcare provider) can make better decisions next time.

Is it a medical device?

No. It's an informational tool. It's not FDA-cleared and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Always work with a qualified healthcare professional for diabetes management.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. The app uses Apple's SwiftData for meal entries and the iOS Keychain for your Dexcom Share credentials. Nothing is sent to any server we run — we don't have one.

The only thing that leaves your device is the request to Dexcom's servers (when you have Dexcom Share connected) to fetch your glucose readings. That communication is between your phone and Dexcom directly.

Do I need a Dexcom to use this app?

A Dexcom Share connection unlocks the automatic glucose-tracking feature. You can still log meals + bolus units without it, but the 3-hour glucose response chart depends on Dexcom data.

Does it work with FreeStyle Libre / Medtronic / other CGMs?

Right now: Dexcom Share only. Other CGM integrations are on the roadmap based on demand. If you want this, email me — it helps me prioritize.

How do I connect my Dexcom?

Settings → Connect Dexcom. Enter your Dexcom Share username and password (the credentials you use for the Dexcom Share / Follow app). The app authenticates with Dexcom's servers and stores those credentials encrypted in the iOS Keychain. You can disconnect any time.

Does the app work with insulin pumps?

The app tracks the units you tell it you bolused. It doesn't read directly from any pump. Manual entry works for pump or pen users equally.

Can I edit a past meal entry?

Tap the entry in the Journal to view it. You can delete entries via the swipe gesture in the Journal list. Editing existing entries is on the roadmap.

How do I export my data?

In the Journal, tap the share icon in the top right. The app generates a CSV file of all your meal entries — you can share it via Mail, AirDrop, save to Files, whatever you choose.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone. That permanently removes all locally-stored data, including any Keychain entries. The app doesn't sync to iCloud, so reinstalling will start you fresh.

What about my data if I get a new phone?

Currently the app stores everything locally on the device it's installed on. If you migrate phones via Apple's standard backup-and-restore (iCloud backup or direct device-to-device transfer), your meal history should come with you. If you set the phone up as new, the history won't transfer — there's no cloud sync.

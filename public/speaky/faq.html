FAQ

The voice is mispronouncing a name. How do I fix it?

Works for any name — your character's, your own, a friend's. Spell it the way it sounds, not the way it's actually spelled. The voice engine reads what it sees, so unusual spellings trip it up.

Examples:
"Aoife" → EE-fa or Eefa
"Siobhan" → Shi-VON or Shavon
Your name is "Sean" but it says "Seen" → type it Shawn
Sound-it-out spellings always win.
What's the difference between Kid mode and Adult mode?

Kid mode keeps everything magical and safe — gentle voices, friendly personalities, nothing scary or crude. Adult mode unlocks the full personality catalog (the absurd, the political, the unhinged).

How do I get more talk time?

Every plan refills monthly. You can also buy a one-time top-up of minutes anytime from the Settings screen — no commitment.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings inside Speaky, tap your plan, and tap Cancel. It opens Apple's Manage Subscriptions screen. You keep any unused minutes, but no new minutes will refill.

Does Speaky save my voice recordings?

Audio is transcribed in real time and discarded. We don't keep recordings of you. See the Privacy Policy for the full breakdown.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. Open Settings → Account → Delete account. Everything tied to you (characters, usage, conversation history) is removed.

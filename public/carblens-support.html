Common questions

What does CarbLens actually do?

You snap a photo of your meal, enter how many units you bolused, and the app pulls your blood glucose from Dexcom for the next 3 hours. The journal shows you, for every meal you logged, what happened to your sugar afterward. Over time you build a real memory of how foods affect you.

My Dexcom won't connect.

Open Settings inside the app → Connect to Dexcom. Use the same username and password you use for the Dexcom Share / Follow app. If you can log in there and still can't here, email me and I'll dig in.

Does the app tell me what to bolus?

No. It's a logging and tracking tool, not a medical device. It shows what happened so you (with your healthcare provider) can decide better next time.

Do I need a Dexcom?

A Dexcom Share connection is what powers the automatic 3-hour glucose chart. You can still log meals and bolus units without it, but the response chart depends on Dexcom data.

What about FreeStyle Libre, Medtronic, or other CGMs?

Right now: Dexcom Share only. Other integrations are on the roadmap — email me if you want one prioritized.

Can I edit a past meal entry?

You can delete entries by swiping on them in the Journal. Editing existing entries is on the roadmap.

How do I export my data?

In the Journal, tap the share icon in the top right. The app generates a CSV file of all your meals — you can send it via Mail, AirDrop, save to Files, whatever you want.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone. Everything is stored locally — uninstalling removes it for good. The app doesn't sync to iCloud.

I want a refund — there's no refund, right?

CarbLens is free. Nothing to refund.

Privacy?

Your meal log and Dexcom credentials stay on your device — I don't have a server. The only thing that leaves your phone is the request to Dexcom's own servers when you have Dexcom Share connected. Full details in the Privacy Policy.

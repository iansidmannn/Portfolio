Support

Need a hand with Vibe? Most answers are below — and if not, the button at the bottom goes straight to us.

How do I enable the Vibe keyboard?

Open your iPhone's Settings app, then:

Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → Vibe.
Then tap Vibe in the keyboard list and turn on Allow Full Access.

After that, hold the globe key on any keyboard and pick Vibe to switch to it.

What is "Allow Full Access" used for?

Full Access is what lets the keyboard reach our servers so the coaches can actually respond. The keyboard only reads the text in your message field when you tap a Vibe action, and only sends it then. It never logs keystrokes, never collects what you type passively, and never transmits anything in the background. Full breakdown in the Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel Vibe Pro?

Vibe Pro is billed through your Apple ID, so cancelling happens in Apple's settings: open the Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → Vibe → Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for.

I bought Pro (or a character) but the app says I didn't?

Open Vibe's settings and tap Restore Purchases. That re-checks your Apple ID and unlocks everything you've bought — handy after a reinstall or on a new phone.

How many free chats do I get?

The free tier gives you a limited number of coach chats each week. Vibe Pro ($4.99/month) makes them unlimited.

Do you save my conversations or screenshots?

Your content is processed to generate the coach's response and isn't kept as a profile of you. No accounts, no ads, nothing sold, ever. See the Privacy Policy for the full breakdown.

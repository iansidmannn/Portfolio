Privacy Policy

Effective: July 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Viral Builders ("we", "us") handles information when you use the Vibe mobile application, its custom keyboard, and its iMessage extension (together, "Vibe" or the "App"). Short version: there are no accounts, your settings live on your phone, and your content goes to our servers only when you ask a coach for help.

1. No accounts

Vibe has no sign-up and no accounts. We don't ask for your name, email, or phone number, and we have no user database with a profile of you in it.

2. What stays on your device

Your settings and preferences — chosen coaches, options, purchase state — are stored on your device in App Group shared storage, which the main app, the keyboard, and the iMessage extension share so they all stay in sync. This data never leaves your phone except as part of a coaching request you make (below).

3. What we process when you ask for coaching

When you request coaching — tap React, Ask, a suggestion pill, or generate replies — the text you typed and/or the screenshot you attached is sent over HTTPS to our backend (Supabase edge functions), which forwards it to our AI providers to generate the result:

  • Anthropic (Claude) — analyzes your conversation and writes the coach's feedback and suggested messages.
  • Supabase — the server infrastructure that relays your request and the response.
  • ElevenLabs — generates character voice audio for coaches that speak.

Your content is processed to answer your request — that's it. We don't use it to train models, we don't sell it, and we don't show ads.

4. The keyboard and Full Access

The Vibe keyboard needs Full Access to talk to our servers. Here's exactly what that means in practice:

  • The keyboard only reads the text in the current message field when you tap a Vibe action, and sends it for processing only then.
  • It does not log keystrokes.
  • It does not collect what you type passively.
  • It does not transmit anything in the background.

If you never tap a Vibe button, nothing you type ever leaves your phone.

5. Purchases

Vibe Pro and character purchases are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your card details. The App checks your purchase status with Apple to unlock what you've bought.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your coaching content is processed transiently — it's used to generate the response and isn't kept as a profile of you. Your settings live on your device and disappear when you uninstall the App. For any privacy request — deletion, questions, anything — email viralbuildersllc@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information. Since we don't keep accounts, there's usually nothing on our side to pull — but if you want to exercise these rights or just confirm that, email viralbuildersllc@gmail.com.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures (TLS encryption, access controls) to protect your information in transit and on our servers. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Age

Vibe is for users 17 and up. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us at viralbuildersllc@gmail.com.

10. International Users

Vibe is operated from the United States. By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your information to the U.S. and other countries where our service providers operate.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top reflects the most recent changes. Material changes will be communicated in the App.

12. Contact

Questions or requests? Email is our only direct contact channel: viralbuildersllc@gmail.com.
